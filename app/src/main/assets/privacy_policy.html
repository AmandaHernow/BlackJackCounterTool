Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Android app information

The counter and these supporting pages are bundled with the Android app and load locally. Card entries and counting sessions remain in the current WebView page; the optional theme preference is stored in WebView local storage. The app does not upload counting data or request Internet permission. External websites and email apps are opened only when you select their links and are handled by separate apps.

The Android app is a separate implementation. Android-specific storage and navigation details are summarized above; the remaining policy sections describe the static browser version of Blackjack Counter Tool hosted on GitHub Pages and its self-contained HTML file.

Website delivery and hosting

When you open the hosted web page, your browser connects to GitHub Pages to request the site files. GitHub provides the hosting service and may process request data under its own policies. This initial delivery is separate from the counter's calculations.

You can also download the web project, including its local image assets, and open BJCounterTool.html from that folder. Counting calculations run in the browser and do not require network requests. Keeping the assets/ folder alongside the HTML file also makes the local QR code, developer photograph, and project icons available offline.

Counting data and browser storage

The web counter does not upload your cards, running count, shoe state, action log, or counting sessions. Calculations and action-log display happen locally in the current browser page. No account is required.

The app stores only your optional dark or light theme preference in browser local storage under the key bj-theme. This preference remains on your device unless you clear browser storage. Counting-session state is not saved to local storage.

Analytics, advertising, and third parties

The web application contains no analytics or behavioral-tracking scripts, advertising trackers, third-party SDKs, or remotely loaded fonts and images. The local PayPal QR image does not contact PayPal when the page loads.

Links to GitHub, related projects, email, and PayPal are user-initiated. If you choose to open PayPal, PayPal handles that visit under its own privacy practices. Your browser or email provider may also handle a mail link according to its settings.

Permissions and children

The browser version does not request device permissions. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the web application's data handling or this policy changes, the updated policy will be published at this address with a revised date.
